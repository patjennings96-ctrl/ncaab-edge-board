      Rules

      Edge Board

      • Spread plays need 2+ points of disagreement with the consensus line; totals need 3+.
      • Books shopped: DraftKings, FanDuel, theScore Bet, BetMGM, Bally Bet, Hard Rock, BetRivers, Kalshi and Polymarket (prediction-market prices converted to American odds, Kalshi's fee included). The best book is the one with the highest expected value, weighing line and price together.
      • Every play risks enough to win 1u at the price taken: 1.1u at −110, 1.05u at −105, 1u at even money.
      • Tracking only for now: Edge Board plays are posted and graded, not staked. When staking is turned on, it runs Jan 1 – Mar 8 only.
      • Spreads of 15+ points are not bet and not shown; they are still graded in the background so the cap can be reviewed.
      • Totals more than 5 points off the market are tracked, not bet, even then: last season those lost.
      • Totals that Cal's model also picks are sized to win 1.5u (1.65u at −110), graded that way while tracking.
      • Spread sizing: flat to win 1u. From Dec 1, spreads switch to ¼ Kelly on the calibrated win probability (cap 3u) if 300+ graded plays have won at the rate the model predicted.
      • Play to is the worst line that still meets these rules at −110. Past it, pass.

      Cal's model

      • Two projections per game (possession × efficiency, and adjusted ratings), each with a 3-point home edge.
      • A spread play needs both models 5+ points off the line on the same side; a total needs 7+.
      • Every play risks 1.1u to win 1u. Cal's rules have no betting window.
      • Play to is the worst line at which both models still clear their thresholds.

      Updates

      • Morning run at 5:15 AM Arizona time: new results, ratings, injuries, today's slate.
      • Lines re-priced at 9:15 AM, 12:15, 3:15 and 5:15 PM, and whenever new injury news is logged.
      • Plays show live scores and the live in-game spread and total (DraftKings live odds via ESPN) while games are on, refreshed every minute, and grade themselves when they end. The season record catches up within about 20 minutes, 10 AM – 12:40 AM.
