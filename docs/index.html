Rules

Edge Board

  • Spread plays need 2+ points of disagreement with the consensus line; totals need 3+.
  • Spread stakes are ¼ Kelly on a win probability calibrated to the backtest (about 54% at a 3-point gap), max 3u (1u = 1% of bankroll), sized at the best number across books.
  • Totals are 1u flat. Totals more than 5 points off the market are tracked, not bet: last season those lost.
  • Staked Jan 1 – Mar 8 only. Before and after, plays are posted and graded as tracking only (0u).
  • Totals that Cal's model also picks get 1.5u.
  • Play to is the worst line that still meets these rules at −110. Past it, pass.

Cal's model

  • Two projections per game (possession × efficiency, and adjusted ratings), each with a 3-point home edge.
  • A spread play needs both models 5+ points off the line on the same side; a total needs 7+.
  • 1u flat per play; wins pay +1u, losses −1.1u. Cal's rules have no betting window.
  • Play to is the worst line at which both models still clear their thresholds.

Updates

  • Morning run at 5:15 AM Arizona time: new results, ratings, injuries, today's slate.
  • Lines re-priced at 9:15 AM, 12:15, 3:15 and 5:15 PM, and whenever new injury news is logged.
  • Results and closing-line value are graded automatically after games finish.
