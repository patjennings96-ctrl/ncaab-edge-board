Core metric

Adjusted efficiency

Possessions and pace

Poss = FGA − OREB + TOV + 0.475·FTA Tempo = Poss · 40 / minutes RawOE = 100 · PointsFor / Poss

Each game's possession count averages both teams' estimates. Per-100 scoring removes pace, so a slow grinder and a fast team are compared on quality, not volume.

Opponent adjustment

RawOE = μ + off[team] + def[opp] + hca·home AdjO = μ + off AdjD = μ + def AdjEM = AdjO − AdjD

One ridge regression over every game, refit using only games before the prediction date. The ridge penalty doubles as early-season shrinkage.

Game projection

Poss = tempo_H · tempo_A / league_tempo Pts_home = (μ + off_H + def_A + hca) · Poss / 100 Margin = Pts_home − Pts_away Total = Pts_home + Pts_away

Early-season prior

  • Regress last year's AdjEM toward the mean, by 0.55 + 0.30 × continuity.
  • Add about 2.5 points per standard deviation of incoming recruiting and transfer talent.
  • Blend in current data at weight n / (n + 12) games played.
Feature store

What adjusts the projection

Rest & travel
Days rest, back-to-backs, games in the last 7 days, consecutive road games, haversine miles between venues, and altitude relative to home (Air Force, Wyoming, Colorado, Utah, New Mexico).
Continuity
Share of last season's minutes returning. In the transfer-portal era this separates teams that keep their rating from teams that reset.
Availability
Usage-weighted share of the offense available tonight. Losing a 28%-usage star costs far more than losing a reserve.
Home court
Per-team HCA, shrunk toward the league mean: (n·observed + 25·league) / (n + 25). Strong venues earn their own number only with enough home games.
Recent form
EWMA of game efficiency margin (half-life 5 games) minus the season baseline, shifted so a game never sees its own result.
Modeling

Two models, one ensemble each

Spreads

Margin model

Predicts home margin. Margin responds to strength gaps, rest and home court more than to pace.

Totals

Total model

Predicts combined points. Totals move with pace and two-sided defense, which barely changes the margin.

  • Gradient-boosted trees (LightGBM, sklearn fallback) catch interactions such as a tired team at altitude against a fast opponent.
  • Bayesian Ridge anchors the blend. With noise around 10–11 points per game, a regularized linear model is hard to beat and rarely overfits.
  • Huber loss limits the pull of blowouts. Quantile heads at the 16th and 84th percentiles give the σ that turns a projection into a cover probability.

Backtesting

How the model earns trust

Walk-forward validation only. For every game on date D, ratings, features and model weights come from games before D. The common silent bug is fitting full-season ratings and then backtesting past games with them, which leaks the future into every projection.
